Dansk film fra Asta Nielsen til Druk

Every director, actor and film named in the official study material, in time order. The year on the left is the year of the film the material highlights first.

Prizes at a glance

  1. 1987
    Bille August · Oscar + Palme d'Or
    Pelle Erobreren (1987), based on Martin Andersen Nexø's novel. The material gives the film's year, not the award year.
  2. 1988
    Gabriel Axel · Oscar
    Babettes Gæstebud (1987). The first Danish director to win an Oscar.
  3. 1998
    Thomas Vinterberg · Prix du Jury, Cannes
    Festen (1998).
  4. 2000
    Lars von Trier · Palme d'Or
    Dancer in the Dark (2000), with the Icelandic singer Björk in the lead.
  5. 2010
    Susanne Bier · Oscar + Golden Globe
    Hævnen (2010). The material gives the film's year.
  6. 2012
    Mads Mikkelsen · Best actor, Cannes
    For the role of Lucas in Jagten (2012).
  7. 2021
    Thomas Vinterberg · Oscar
    Druk (2020).

To remember: The material names four Oscar-winning directors: Gabriel Axel (the first), Bille August, Susanne Bier and Thomas Vinterberg. Bille August and Lars von Trier both won the Palme d'Or in Cannes.
