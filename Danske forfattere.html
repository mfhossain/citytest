Every author named in the official study material (August 2026), in time order. The year on the left is the year of the work the material highlights. Badges show prizes, Nobel nominations, and film adaptations of their work that won prizes.

Prizes at a glance

  1. 1917
    Henrik Pontoppidan · Nobel Prize in Literature
    Known for Lykke-Per (1898–1904).
  2. 1944
    Johannes V. Jensen · Nobel Prize in Literature
    Known for Kongens Fald (1901) and Himmerlandshistorier.
  3. —
    Inger Christensen · Nobel candidate
    "Several times named a strong candidate" for the Nobel Prize. She never won it.
  4. 1987
    Martin Andersen Nexø · Film of his novel: Oscar + Palme d'Or
    Bille August's film Pelle Erobreren (1987) won both prizes. The material gives the film's year, not the award year.
  5. 1988
    Karen Blixen · Film of her story: Oscar
    Gabriel Axel's Babettes Gæstebud (1987) won the Oscar in 1988, the first Danish director to win one.
  6. 2021
    Niviaq Korneliussen (Greenland) · Nordisk Råds Litteraturpris
    For Naasuliardarpi (Blomsterdalen, 2020).
  7. 2025
    Vónbjørt Vang (Faroe Islands) · Nordisk Råds Litteraturpris
    For the poetry collection Svørt Okidé (Sort Okidé).

To remember: the material names two Danish Nobel laureates in literature, Pontoppidan (1917) and Johannes V. Jensen (1944). Inger Christensen was only a candidate.
