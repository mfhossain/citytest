Every painter and visual artist named in the official study material, in time order. The year on the left is the year of the work the material highlights. A dash means the material gives no year.

Works and records at a glance

  1. 1875
    Skagensmalerne · Artist group
    P.S. Krøyer, Anna Ancher and Michael Ancher. Skagen was an international summer meeting place for painters c. 1875–1910.
  2. 1900
    Vilhelm Hammershøi · Most expensive Danish painting ever
    Interiør fra Strandgade 30 sold at auction in the USA in 2023 for 62.8 million kroner.
  3. 1948
    Asger Jorn · Artist group
    Co-founded Cobra (1948–51), named after Copenhagen, Brussels and Amsterdam. Main work: Stalingrad.
  4. 1969
    Lene Adler Petersen and Bjørn Nørgaard · Action art
    Den kvindelige Kristus: she walked naked through Børsen in Copenhagen carrying a cross.
  5. 2000
    Bjørn Nørgaard · Royal commission
    17 tapestries in Riddersalen at Christiansborg. A gift for the Queen's 50th birthday in 1990, inaugurated on her 60th in 2000.
  6. 2011
    Olafur Eliasson · 150 metres long
    Your rainbow panorama, a circular glass walkway on top of ARoS in Aarhus.

To remember: Hammershøi painted light and shadow in the rooms of a home. The Skagen painters painted the light from two seas, Skagerrak and Kattegat. Jorn's Stalingrad is white-grey like snow with red splashes for blood.
