Anthems and Eurovision at a glance

  1. 1778
    Kong Christian stod ved højen mast · National anthem + royal anthem
    Text by Johannes Ewald (1778/79). The music is part of Friedrich Kuhlau's Elverhøi (1828).
  2. 1819
    Der er et yndigt land · National anthem
    Text by Adam Oehlenschläger (1819), music by Hans Ernst Krøyer (1835).
  3. 1963
    Grethe and Jørgen Ingmann · Eurovision winner
    Dansevise. Denmark's first win.
  4. 2000
    Brødrene Olsen · Eurovision winner
    Smuk som et stjerneskud / Fly on the Wings of Love, written and performed by Jørgen and Niels Olsen.
  5. 2013
    Emmelie de Forest · Eurovision winner
    Only Teardrops.
  6. 1990s
    Aqua · About 28 million CDs sold
    One of the best-selling Danish music groups ever. Barbie Girl was a global hit.

To remember: Denmark is one of the only countries in the world with two national anthems, and it has won the Eurovision Song Contest three times: 1963, 2000 and 2013.
