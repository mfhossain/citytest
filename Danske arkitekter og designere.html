Every architect, designer and building named in the official study material, in time order. The year on the left is the year of the building or design. A dash means the material gives no year.

Who designed what

  1. 1811
    C.F. Hansen · Classicism
    Vor Frue Kirke, Copenhagen's cathedral, built 1811–29.
  2. 1931
    Aarhus Universitet · Functionalism
    Built 1931–33 as a kind of academic village.
  3. 1950s
    Jørn Utzon · Abroad
    The opera house in Sydney, designed in the late 1950s.
  4. —
    Arne Jacobsen · Functionalism
    Danmarks Nationalbank and Aarhus Rådhus. The chairs 7'eren and Ægget.
  5. 2005
    Henning Larsen · Copenhagen landmark
    Operahuset: 41,000 square metres on 14 floors.
  6. 2011
    Bjarke Ingels Group (BIG) · World's best housing project 2011
    8TALLET in Ørestad on Amager. Also Amager Bakke (Copenhill), 2019.

To remember: Functionalism arose in the 1920s and puts practical function first. Removing ornament made houses and furniture more alike, and its political aim was more social equality. PH's lamps and Børge Mogensen's furniture are the textbook examples.
