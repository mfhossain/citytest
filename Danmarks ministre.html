To remember: Mette Frederiksen (S) is Prime Minister, Lars Løkke Rasmussen (M) is Foreign Minister, Peter Hummelgaard (S) is Finance Minister and Nicolai Wammen (S) is Justice Minister. One change since the government was formed: on 27 October 2026 Karina Lorentzen Dehnhardt replaced Signe Munk.
